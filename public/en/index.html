Classic Swedish Massage

Swedish massage is the standard type of massage offered at most clinics, fitness centers, spas and wellness centers. Massage therapists typically use creams or oils, drawing from a wide range of techniques. Swedish massage relaxes the muscles throughout the entire body, which is especially useful if you are recovering from an injury. For pain and tension

Therapeutic Massage -  Lomi Lomi 

Lomi Lomi is a Polynesian massage from Hawaii; the term Lomi Lomi means back and forth. It is a unique technique that allows the therapist to massage a large area of the body at once and work along the entire length of the body using circular, vigorous movements with the forearms and palms, all while dancing and moving, working deep into the muscle tissue. The treatment truly creates a sense of spiritual elevation, as if you have disconnected, feeling a sense of floating and release. It helps stimulate blood circulation in the body, release and relieve tension. 
Want to leave your massage feeling ready to conquer the world? Go for Lomi Lomi – an exotic massage that opens blockages, dissolves tension, and challenges the senses. Designed for people suffering from back, neck, or shoulder pain, or chronic muscle tension. The treatment releases pressure points, improves blood flow, and restores the body to free, pain-free movement.

central and important. Tantra views the body as an essential tool for reaching higher spiritual awareness. Here are some
of the key roles of the body in this process:

  • Mind-Body
    Connection: Tantra emphasizes the relationship between body and mind. Practices such as yoga and meditation help release
    blocking energies and open energetic channels in the body, leading to heightened self-awareness.
    Physical
    Experiences: The body is a vessel for experiencing the world fully. Tantra encourages sensory experiences designed
    to deepen the connection with your environment and yourself. These experiences include physical practices, rituals, and interactions
    with others.
  • Embracing Emotions:
    In the tantric process, the body accepts all emotions, including painful or difficult ones, as part of the spiritual journey.
    Dealing with these emotions through the body allows for personal growth and a deeper understanding of yourself.
  • Intimacy and Relationships: Tantra views the body as a means of building deep connections with others. Practices that involve physical
    and emotional intimacy strengthen interpersonal bonds and allow for genuine connection.
  • Boosting Vital Energy: Tantra focuses on the release and flow of vital energy within the body. Physical practices such as breathwork, postures, and meditation help strengthen this flow, leading
    to profound spiritual experiences.

For a Perfect Experience

Relaxation Massage 

A perfect blend of classic Swedish massage and Lomi Lomi — an uplifting, soothing, and indulgent experience that envelops the entire body, leaving a feeling of floating and complete release.

The combination of classic Swedish massage and Lomi Lomi creates an experience that many therapists and clients describe as deeper, more enveloping, and more balanced than either method alone.
The essence: Swedish provides stability, muscle release, and structure; Lomi Lomi adds flow, softness, and an almost ceremonial sense of calm. Together they create a treatment that is both physically effective and emotionally nourishing.

The key benefits of the combination:
1. A perfect balance between technique and a sense of flow
2. Deep muscle release without strain or pain
3. A soothing and containing emotional experience
4. A perfect fit for those seeking both treatment and indulgence
5. Significant improvement in blood and lymph circulation
6. A feeling of mental tension "melting away"
